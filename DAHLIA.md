# Dahlia Avatar apps

The companion apps for Dahlia: chat with her through an avatar on the web,
on the desktop, and on Android.

They are based on [Project AIRI](https://github.com/moeru-ai/airi) by Moeru AI
(MIT license, see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE)), adapted to Dahlia.

## Get the apps

Download the latest release from
[AleytiaFairlight/Dahlia-avatar](https://github.com/AleytiaFairlight/Dahlia-avatar/releases):
the Windows installer and the Android APK.

## Learn more

Guides and help: [dahlia.aleytia.moe](https://dahlia.aleytia.moe).
