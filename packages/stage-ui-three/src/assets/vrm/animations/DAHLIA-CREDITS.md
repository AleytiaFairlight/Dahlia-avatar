# Animation credits

The `.vrma` files in this folder other than `idle_loop.vrma` come with Dahlia's avatar animation library. They were
taken unchanged from the AIRI fork [dasilva333/airi](https://github.com/dasilva333/airi) (commit 24c706d9d, folder
`packages/stage-ui-three/src/assets/vrm/animations`), whose `CREDITS.md` describes them as follows. They are not
Dahlia's or AIRI's work, and all rights stay with their creators.

They are included for **personal, non-commercial use** of Dahlia's avatar only. Don't sell them, don't pass the
files on separately, and don't use them in anything commercial.
